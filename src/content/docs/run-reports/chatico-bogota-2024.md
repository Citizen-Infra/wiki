---
title: "Chatico in Bogotá's development-plan consultation (2024)"
description: A city chatbot collected policy priorities, with an official snapshot separating contributions from the people who submitted them.
---

Bogotá used **Chatico**, its municipal chatbot, as a channel for residents to contribute to the city's development plan in 2024. Alongside surveys, listening sessions and local meetings, residents could use a familiar messaging interface to select priorities or submit their own proposals. The [People Powered case study](https://www.peoplepowered.org/news-content/digital-participation-case-study-bogota) describes this use alongside separate budgeting and assembly-recruitment campaigns.

## What the dated record shows

An [official snapshot dated 17 April 2024](https://sdmujer.gov.co/sites/default/files/2024-07/archivos-adjunto-pagina/reporte-participacion-chatico-PDD-2024.pdf) records:

| Measure | Reported figure |
|---|---|
| Contributions through Chatico | **147,422** |
| People completing at least one contribution through Chatico | **41,801** |
| Inputs across all channels in the plan's first two participation phases | **216,599** |

The snapshot classifies **90%** of Chatico contributions as choices from a list and **10%** as solutions written by citizens. These figures describe different units: one person could submit more than one contribution. The date and channel are part of what each count measures.

## The chatbot's job

In the case study, Chatico's participation flows explain a campaign, ask structured questions, accept choices or free-text input and confirm submissions. This gives residents another route into a city process, while organisers can analyse the resulting priorities and contributions.

The chronology matters when discussing AI. The city's [2025 ICT report, page 20](https://secretariageneral.gov.co/sites/default/files/documentos_ppi/2025-11/Informe_Dialogo_Ciudadano_Bogot%C3%A1_Capital_Digital_2025_0.pdf#page=20), says generative AI was incorporated into Chatico in **2025**, in collaboration with **ETB**. The [January 2026 public update](https://bogota.gov.co/mi-ciudad/gestion-publica/chatico-agente-virtual-de-bogota-se-renueva-con-ia-generativa-2026) describes its renewed conversational service. Those later capabilities should be assessed separately from the 2024 consultation results.

## Participation and influence

People Powered's interviewees acknowledge continuing barriers involving phone and internet access, reading and writing. The wider consultation used community-based channels as well as digital input. An easy messaging interface can widen the available routes, while those other formats remain necessary.

The published tally documents contributions and participation. It does not show which individual proposals became funded actions, whether the respondents represented the city, or what extra participation was caused by AI. The case illustrates [virtual assistance within a participation process](/concepts/ai-for-participation/); tracing public input into decisions remains a separate task.
