title: "Voice Matters People's Panel (Derry/Londonderry, 2025)"
description: A local citizens' panel discussed community safety, using human facilitation and AI-assisted records that participants could check.
---

In April 2025, **Co-operation Ireland** convened a People's Panel in Derry/Londonderry to ask how the city could become safer and more inclusive. The event was part of **Voice Matters**, its community peacebuilding programme. The [organiser's account](https://cooperationireland.org/news/derrylondonderry-based-citizens-assembly-releases-2025-report) records meetings over two weekends at St Columb's Hall.

## Who took part

The organiser sent invitations to **10,500 households** across three neighbourhoods and selected **30 residents** from respondents through a civic lottery, balancing demographic characteristics. Participants were paid for their time. This was a local [sortition](/concepts/sortition/) process; the invitation count is not an attendance figure. [Source: Co-operation Ireland](https://cooperationireland.org/news/derrylondonderry-based-citizens-assembly-releases-2025-report).

## How people checked the record

The [People Powered case study](https://www.peoplepowered.org/news-content/digital-participation-case-study-ireland) describes three days of learning, deliberation and decisions. Residents heard different perspectives, discussed safety-related issues in small groups, then refined and voted on recommendations.

**Dembrane's ECHO** recorded discussions and generated transcripts, table summaries and cross-table themes. Facilitators reviewed the drafts, and participants saw emerging findings through recaps, table rotations and printed summaries. They could correct omissions or misinterpretations before summaries were finalised. The AI-assisted record supported human facilitation rather than deciding the recommendations.

Co-operation Ireland supplied devices and explained recording and consent. Participants could pause recording, and a **human interpreter** supported an Arabic-speaking participant. Images of possible neighbourhood futures were made separately using Midjourney and Google Maps material. These arrangements are described in the organisers' interviews in the case study.

## Outputs and limits

Co-operation Ireland [published the findings in November 2025](https://cooperationireland.org/news/derrylondonderry-based-citizens-assembly-releases-2025-report), reporting recommendations on employment, mental health provision and cross-community housing. People Powered also describes proposals for integrated education.

Interviewees reported greater openness and easier capture of discussions. These are practitioner and participant observations; the cited accounts do not establish a comparative effect of AI or verify policy implementation.

The practical lesson is the feedback step: residents could inspect the interpretation of their words before it became the collective record. That is a concrete use of [AI sensemaking](/concepts/ai-sensemaking/) within [AI-assisted deliberation](/concepts/ai-and-deliberation/). See [Dembrane in the toolkit](/toolkit/decide-together/) for the platform.
